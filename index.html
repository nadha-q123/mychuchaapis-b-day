Happy birthday to the person who holds my whole heart. ❤️

I honestly have no words to fill this letter. Writing this to you is probably one of the most emotional moments of my life.

You were the only person who stood by me, even when you were mentally tired yourself. You were the safest place I could find, and you became everything to me.

Even after all the fights and everything we’ve been through, you are still my safest place, and somehow, I love you even more.

I still remember my first impression of you — “Aww, such a sweet guy.” And honestly, that impression never changed. 🥹

I’m sorry for all the times I’ve been angry at you and for the things I may have said or done. But today is your day, and all I want is for you to be happy.

So, so many happy returns of the day! May God bless you with everything you deserve and fill your life with happiness, love, and beautiful moments.

Thank you for being exactly who you are. Thank you for being there for me and for being my safest place through everything.

I love you so, so, so much, Chuchapi. ❤️

Happy birthday once again. 🎂

I love you, today and always. ❤️

♡ Naachi
